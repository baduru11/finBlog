---
term: Dot plot
shortDef: The Fed's quarterly chart of where each FOMC participant thinks the policy rate should be at year-end, anonymously plotted as a single dot per person.
category: monetary-policy
---

Published in the Summary of Economic Projections (SEP) four times a year, the dot plot shows the policy rate each FOMC participant thinks would be appropriate at the end of the current year, the next two years, and the longer run.

It is *not* a forecast of what the Fed will do — it is a snapshot of what each participant *thinks should happen* given their own outlook. The market reads the median dot most closely, but the *dispersion* (how spread out the dots are) is often the more revealing signal: a tight cluster means the committee agrees, a wide spread means they don't.
