---
term: MOVE index
shortDef: The ICE BofAML MOVE Index is to Treasuries what the VIX is to equities — an option-implied measure of expected one-month volatility on U.S. Treasury futures, weighted across the 2-, 5-, 10-, and 30-year tenors.
category: rates
---

When the MOVE rises, dealers in Treasury options are charging more for tail-risk protection, which usually shows up before realized rate volatility actually picks up. Historically the index hovered in the 50–80 range; after the 2022 rate cycle it shifted to a 100–140 regime as fiscal supply, Fed-cycle uncertainty, and bank-balance-sheet capacity all became more contested.

A useful read of the MOVE is as a proxy for *option-market term premium*: when MOVE is low while nominal yields are high, the bond market is implicitly pricing the high yields as a stable equilibrium rather than a transition.
