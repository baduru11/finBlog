---
term: Supercore inflation
shortDef: Services inflation excluding both shelter and energy. The Fed's preferred read on the part of inflation it can actually influence with monetary policy.
category: macro
---

The Fed targets headline PCE inflation at 2%. But headline includes food, energy, shelter, and services. Energy is supply-driven and the Fed doesn't control oil. Shelter operates on multi-year lease cycles and lags real rents by 12–18 months. That leaves services-ex-shelter — "supercore" — as the cleanest read on the part of inflation actually responsive to monetary policy.

Supercore is where wage growth, labor-market tightness, and inflation expectations show up. When supercore is running at 4%+, the Fed is not done. When it falls below 3%, the underlying disinflation is real even if headline is noisy. The measure exists in both CPI and PCE flavours; they usually agree but can diverge for a quarter or two on weighting differences.
