---
term: Real yields
shortDef: The yield on a bond minus expected inflation — what you actually earn after the purchasing-power loss from inflation.
category: rates
---

A nominal yield is the headline number a bond pays. A real yield is that number minus expected inflation over the same horizon. In the US, real yields are observed directly through Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS) — a 10-year TIPS yield *is* the 10-year real yield.

Real yields matter because they are the true cost of capital for the economy. When real yields are negative, debtors are effectively being paid to borrow; when they are sharply positive, financial conditions are tight regardless of what the nominal rate is. The 10-year real yield is one of the cleanest single indicators of how restrictive monetary policy actually is.
