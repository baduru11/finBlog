---
term: Nonfarm payrolls
shortDef: The monthly count of US net job creation excluding farm work, military, and a few other categories. The single most-watched labor-market data point in finance.
category: macro
---

Released by the BLS on the first Friday of every month at 8:30 ET, the Employment Situation report contains two headline numbers: the change in nonfarm payrolls (NFP) and the unemployment rate. NFP comes from a survey of roughly 144,000 businesses and government agencies (the establishment survey); unemployment comes from a smaller survey of households.

The two surveys often disagree, sometimes sharply, and the difference is informative — it can tell you whether weakness is in hiring, in workforce participation, or in measurement noise. The headline NFP number is also revised twice in subsequent months and often substantially; markets often move more on revisions than on the initial print. Watch the three-month moving average and the participation rate; the headline alone can mislead in either direction.
